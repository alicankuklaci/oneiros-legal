English

Delete in the app (recommended, immediate)

1. Open Oneiros → Settings → Delete Account. 2. Once you confirm, your account is closed; your email address, all dream entries, tags, and subscription records are permanently erased within 30 days. This cannot be undone.

If you can't access the app (web request)

Email alican.kuklaci@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, using the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We verify that the sender owns the account, complete the deletion within the same 30-day window, and send you a confirmation email.

What is deleted: email address, dream texts and voice transcripts, emotion tags, in-app subscription record. What is not: your purchase history inside App Store/Google Play remains in the stores' own systems (outside our control); anonymous analytics events are not linked to your identity and contain no personal data to delete.

Note: Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel it separately in your App Store account settings or Google Play → Subscriptions.
