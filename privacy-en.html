1. Who We Are

Oneiros is operated by an individual developer ("we"). Contact: alican.kuklaci@gmail.com.

2. What We Collect

  • Email address — for account creation and sign-in (magic link).
  • Dream entries — the text you write, transcripts of voice recordings, and emotion tags you add. Because dream content can be psychological and emotional in nature, we treat it as sensitive personal data and process it only with the explicit consent you give on the consent screen shown before recording (kvkk_v1).
  • Purchase status — to verify subscriptions (your payment card details never reach us; Apple/Google process payment).
  • Anonymous usage analytics (optional) — in-app events not linked to your identity; can be turned off.
  • Device permissions — microphone (voice capture) and notifications; both optional and revocable in device settings.

3. Purposes and Legal Basis

We process data to provide the service (performance of contract), generate AI interpretations (explicit consent), and improve the app (legitimate interest, anonymous analytics only). We do not sell data or process it for advertising.

4. Processors and Transfers

Supabase (hosting, auth, database — EU/US region, encryption at rest + TLS), OpenAI (dream text is sent to generate interpretations; per its API policy it is not used to train models), RevenueCat (subscription status), PostHog (optional anonymous analytics). As servers are in the EU/US, your data is transferred internationally on the basis of your explicit consent.

5. Retention and Deletion

Data is kept until you delete it. You can delete your account in-app (Settings → Delete Account); all dream data is permanently erased within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You may access, correct, delete, object to processing of, and export your data (in-app export). Requests: alican.kuklaci@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local supervisory authority (in Türkiye, the KVKK Board).

7. Children

Oneiros is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in GDPR regions); we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Security and Changes

Data is protected with TLS in transit and encryption at rest. We announce policy changes on this page and, where required, in the app.
